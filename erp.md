TARAND Intelligent ERP is a next-generation Autonomous Enterprise Operating System designed for the realities of African business. Traditional ERPs act as systems of record—passive databases requiring human data entry.

**TARAND is a System of Action.**

## 2. The Problem Landscape: Business in Zimbabwe & Africa

African businesses face a unique combination of challenges that Western ERPs are not designed for.

### A. Currency Volatility & Multi-Currency Chaos

**Problem:** Daily rate volatility between ZiG, USD, ZAR complicates pricing, invoicing, reporting, and reconciliation.

**Gap:** Most ERPs assume a single stable currency; local dual-ledger compliance is difficult.

### B. Connectivity & Infrastructure Gaps

**Problem:** Internet is intermittent, expensive, and unreliable.

**Gap:** Cloud-only systems break when offline. Desktop systems lack mobility.

### C. Regulatory Complexity (ZIMRA & Local Statutes)

**Problem:** Tax regulations change frequently. Compliance is time-consuming.

**Gap:** Foreign ERPs lack ZIMRA logic, statutory updates, or automated compliance engines.

### D. Mobile-First Reality

**Problem:** Most business happens on WhatsApp and mobile phones, not desktops.

**Gap:** ERPs are not optimized for mobile productivity or conversational workflows.

## 3. The Solution: TARAND Intelligent ERP

TARAND is engineered for resilience, automation, and real-world African operations.

### Solution 1: “Zero-Click” AI Operations

TARAND behaves like an intelligent workforce inside the company.

#### AI Accountant
- Reads receipts (OCR)
- Detects currency automatically
- Applies daily or custom exchange rate
- Categorizes transaction
- Posts to ledger without user action

#### AI Collections Agent
- Sends automated WhatsApp or email reminders
- Predicts late payments
- Escalates overdue invoices
- Improves cash flow automatically

#### AI Procurement Agent
- Reads incoming RFQs
- Suggests best suppliers
- Drafts purchase orders
- Tracks procurement timelines

**TARAND reduces manual workload by 60–90% depending on module.**

### Solution 2: PWA Architecture (Offline-First)
- Create invoices offline
- Approve workflows offline
- Conduct stock takes offline
- Field technicians work fully offline
- Sync resumes automatically when online

No app store updates. No downloads. No heavy bandwidth costs.

### Solution 3: Native Multi-Currency Engine
- Dual-ledger
- Automatic exchange gain/loss calculations
- Official + manual street rate support
- Real-time rate pulling
- Consolidated currency reporting
